University of Toledo · Dissertation: Impact of Online Asynchronous Orientation on Student Enrollment, Retention, and GPA for BGSU Online Students. Quantitative correlational study utilizing archival data to analyze the impact of asynchronous eCampus Orientation on enrollment, retention, and first-course GPA of newly admitted adult online learners.

An early institutional case study of distance education at BGSU, arguing — after Negroponte's "being digital is different" — that computer-mediated learning is not simply classroom teaching moved online, and examining what that shift asks of pedagogy, administration, and the learners it aims to reach.

Discoverly — A Discipleship App

Currently in Pilot Phase
Discover the mission in every message.
discoverly.com

Discoverly is a discipleship app I designed to help church members live out the mission of the sermon all week long. Rather than letting Sunday's message fade by Monday morning, Discoverly turns each sermon or homily into weekly missions a congregation can walk out together — in groups, or privately.

The pastor or priest approves every mission before anyone sees it, keeping the content tied to their specific message and context — not a generic devotional. Currently in active pilot phase with partnering churches.
